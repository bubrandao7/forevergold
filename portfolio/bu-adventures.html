The idea

You can be gentle
and still have boundaries.

She is soft, but she knows her worth. For anyone told to choose between being kind and being strong. Bu is an original character at the center of a Pixar-style 3D animated short series. Everything in her world looks soft and gentle, and that is the point. Bu is sweet but not a pushover. She carries a crystal spiked club, a small reminder that kindness does not mean weakness.

Meet the cast

Bu

A chocolate-brown bunny with turquoise eyes, hot-pink whiskers and adorable buck teeth. She lives in the Pink Forest, a calm, candy-colored world of cotton-candy trees, giant polka-dot mushrooms, a tiny house and neat rows of planted carrots.

Why it works

Clear, original, easy to scale.

01

A character you remember

A clear, original character with a story that needs no language. Pink whiskers and a crystal club make Bu easy to recognize on any screen and easy to turn into merchandise.

02

A world built to repeat

The Pink Forest is deliberately minimal, with little visual noise and a tight color palette. Episodes can be produced quickly while the world stays consistent.

03

Fast and low cost

The whole concept, images, animation and narration, was built with accessible AI tools. That means fast iteration and low production cost.
