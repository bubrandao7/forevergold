Case study · Forevergold

Forevergold

A house of gold and silver in Gondomar, with five stores and one name. I redrew its symbol, designed its website and built a working app for its team, all in one language of light and motion.

ClientForevergold, Gondomar
ServicesLogo redraw, website, team app, motion design
DateOct 2026
The house

An old house,five stores, one name.

Forevergold is a trusted house in the Porto region, with its headquarters in Gondomar. It buys and sells gold and silver, lends against jewellery, repairs watches and engraves by laser. The company was growing, and its digital presence needed to look as careful as the work it does.

5

stores to connect under one identity

2

products, a website and a team app

9

app accounts, each with its own permissions

What I made

Four pieces,one house.

01

Logo redraw

The logo only existed as a photo. I redrew the symbol and the wordmark as clean vector artwork.

02

Website

A luxury shop window with orders by WhatsApp, Google reviews and a way for the owner to edit everything.

03

Team app

A working app for five stores: pieces for sale, chat, daily gold quote, a monthly profit game and advertising.

04

Motion

One motion language, gold that draws, fills, orbits and engraves, shared by both products.

Identity

One symbol,redrawn in light.

The symbol stays exactly as the company drew it, and so does the ®. What changed is the lettering, now a high-contrast serif, and the way gold is treated: as a surface that catches the light instead of a flat yellow.

The website

A shop windowthat never closes.

One long page that walks a visitor from trust to action: who the house is, what it buys, how pawn works, what the laser can write, and every piece for sale, with a cart that sends the order straight to WhatsApp.

Orders by WhatsAppReal Google reviewsEditable in place
Motion

Gold isnever still.

Every movement has a job. It signals value, explains a process or proves a craft. Choose a moment and watch it move.

The app

A pocket forthe whole team.

This is the real app, running inside the page. Customers browse the pieces and the five stores, and the team gets its own tools: chat, the daily gold quote, the monthly profit game and advertising.

1

Tap the phone to open the app.

2

“Entrar como cliente” shows what customers see.

3

Or swipe the name to a store and choose any 4-digit code, twice, to enter the team side.

The app is in Portuguese, like its team. Demo data is marked “Exemplo” and stays only in your browser.

If the app does not open here, open it on its own page.

01

Pieces for sale

Photos, price, material, weight and status. Each store edits only its own. Customers call or write on WhatsApp.

02

One team chat

Messages can be marked urgent, and the whole team is notified.

03

Daily gold quote

Price per gram by month, week and day, with a chart. Posting it notifies the team.

04

Monthly profit game

One point per 1,000 €, a season per calendar year, and a bonus point for sharing every ad.

05

Advertising

Images and videos to download, with ready-made text to copy.

06

Accounts and codes

Nine accounts, each person picks a 4-digit code. Customers enter with no code and see only what is public.

Screens

Dark, quiet,precise.

Why it works

Threedecisions.

1

One language everywhere

The site and the app share the same colours, type and motion, so the house feels the same on every screen.

2

Motion with a purpose

Every movement signals value, explains a process or proves a craft.

3

Made to grow

The owner edits the site in place, and the app keeps its data in one isolated layer, ready to connect to a server.
