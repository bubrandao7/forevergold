The person behindthe work (Bu)

Right now, readingDreamer

I’m an ambitious, determined, and creative professional driven by a constant desire to learn, evolve, and turn ideas into meaningful results.

Art has always been an important part of my journey. From an early age, I developed a natural talent and passion for drawing, consistently standing out as one of the strongest students in my art studies. This artistic background shaped my creativity, attention to detail, aesthetics and intuitive way of seeing the world.

I am currently exploring and developing my skills in photography, video editing, design and content creation — areas that genuinely inspire me. I also have a strong passion for social media and its creative potential to tell stories, communicate ideas, build communities, and create a powerful visual identity.

I consider myself a positive, intuitive, and ambitious person with a strong sense of determination. I am not afraid to step outside my comfort zone, embrace new challenges, and continuously seek opportunities for growth. I am now looking to bring together my commercial experience and creative mindset in a professional environment where I can continue learning, contribute with fresh ideas, and build a career that reflects both my skills and my personality.

I’m a dreamer, but the kind who draws the dream and makes it work.
